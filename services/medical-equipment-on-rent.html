Rental Guidelines & Clinical Safety Safeguards

  • Respiratory Devices Require Prescription: Oxygen concentrators and positive airway pressure devices require a clinician's order stating the required liters/minute and target SpO2 levels.
  • Multi-Stage Sanitization: All returned equipment undergoes rigorous antibacterial disinfection and mechanical load testing before re-rental.
  • No Stock Guarantees: Stock levels fluctuate daily. Delivery schedules and model availability are confirmed at the time of your WhatsApp or telephone enquiry.
  • Electrical Stability Notice: For motorized beds and oxygen concentrators, households must ensure standard grounded electrical outlets and inverter backup support.

What Patients Should Know Before Enquiring

  • Measure Doorways & Room Space: For motorized hospital beds, ensure stairwell, elevator, and room entry widths allow delivery clearance.
  • Confirm Rental Duration: Equipment is typically rented on flexible monthly or multi-week cycles.
  • Security Deposit & ID Proof: Standard government identity proof and a refundable security deposit are verified at time of delivery.
