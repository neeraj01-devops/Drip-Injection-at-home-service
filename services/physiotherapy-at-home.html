Detailed Service Overview

Restoring independent movement, relieving chronic pain, and rebuilding joint stability require personalized physical therapy conducted with clinical patience. For patients recovering from total knee replacement (TKR), total hip replacement (THR), stroke rehabilitation, Parkinson's disease, or age-related balance deficits, commuting through Gurugram traffic to an outpatient clinic can trigger severe fatigue or reinjury risk.

Our home physiotherapy sessions are delivered directly at your residence by qualified, degree-holding physiotherapists (BPT / MPT). Each program begins with an objective assessment of range of motion, muscle strength (MMT), gait pattern, balance, and pain scores, forming the basis for goal-oriented therapeutic exercises in your real daily living environment.

Assessment & Clinical Requirements

Doctor / Orthopedic Reference: For post-surgical cases or acute fractures, written clearance and specific weight-bearing protocols from your orthopedic surgeon or neurologist must be reviewed prior to commencing active therapy.
  • Initial Functional Assessment: Goniometric measurement of joint angles, manual muscle testing, sensation mapping, and postural stability review.
  • Therapeutic Modalities: Mobilization techniques, active-assisted exercises, resistance band training, and modalities (such as portable TENS / IFT if clinically indicated).
  • Fall Prevention & Ergonomics: Analyzing bedroom, bathroom, and hallway hazards to advise on grab bars, non-slip mats, and walking frame adjustments.
  • Caregiver Education: Training family members on safe transfer techniques and daily maintenance exercises between visits.

Rehabilitation Domains & Limitations

Rehabilitation Programs:
  • Post-TKR / THR joint rehabilitation
  • Stroke & hemiplegia motor retraining
  • Cervical & lumbar spine pain therapy
  • Geriatric mobility & balance drills
Contraindications to Exercise:
  • •Unstable spinal or unreduced fractures
  • •Acute cardiac angina or severe arrhythmias
  • •Severe acute joint inflammation or septic arthritis
  • •Confirmed unmedicated acute DVT

What Patients Should Know Before Enquiring

  • Have Investigation Reports Handy: Keep X-rays, MRI scans, or surgical discharge notes available for the physiotherapist's initial review.
  • Comfortable Clothing: Ensure the patient wears loose, non-restrictive attire that allows full joint observation and safe movement.
  • Session Scheduling: Sessions are typically 45 minutes in duration, scheduled at convenient morning or evening slots across Gurugram.
