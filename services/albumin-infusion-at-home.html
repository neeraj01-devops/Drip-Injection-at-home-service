Clinical Distinction: Albumin Is Not a Routine Saline Drip

Human Albumin (5% or 20%) is a concentrated human plasma protein derivative with potent oncotic fluid-shifting properties. It is prescribed strictly by gastroenterologists, hepatologists, nephrologists, or critical care physicians for conditions like severe hypoalbuminemia, liver cirrhosis with refractory ascites, or post-paracentesis. It is never a routine hydration drip, wellness infusion, or cosmetic therapy.

Detailed Service Overview

Because albumin draws significant intravascular fluid volume rapidly into circulation, it carries clinical risks of hypervolemia, acute pulmonary edema, hypertension, or allergic hypersensitivity if not administered under stringent medical criteria.

We accept enquiries for prescribed albumin administration in Gurugram strictly on a case-by-case evaluation basis. Each enquiry undergoes multi-point clinical screening: specialist physician authorization review, current renal and cardiac status confirmation, attending nursing qualification verification, and validation that continuous hemodynamic monitoring equipment is in place.

Prescription & Clinical Assessment Requirements

  • Specialist Physician Prescription: An explicit order from the treating specialist (Gastroenterologist, Hepatologist, Nephrologist, or Intensivist) detailing the concentration (5% or 20%), dosage, infusion duration (e.g. 100ml over 1 to 2 hours), and emergency protocol.
  • Pre-Therapy Cardiovascular & Respiratory Screening: Auscultation of lung sounds to rule out baseline crackles (pulmonary congestion), baseline pulse, blood pressure, and SpO2.
  • Ventilated Infusion Spike & Vented Set: Albumin is packaged in rigid glass bottles requiring a dedicated vented IV infusion administration set with in-line filter.
  • Continuous Bedside Vitals Monitoring: Periodic blood pressure, pulse, and oxygen saturation readings every 15 minutes during infusion.

Service Scope & Safety Limitations

Administration will NOT proceed in the home setting under any of the following clinical conditions:

Strict Home Contraindications (Hospitalization Advised):
  • •Severe decompensated heart failure or active pulmonary edema
  • •Uncontrolled arterial hypertension
  • •History of severe anaphylactic allergic reactions to human albumin
  • •Absence of a direct physician escalation contact

What Patients Should Know Before Enquiring

  • Cold-Chain Verification: Human Albumin must be stored between 2°C and 25°C and not frozen. Keep the bottle in its official packaging until the nurse arrives.
  • Specialist Contact Details: Have your treating hospital / doctor's contact number readily available for clinical liaison if required.
  • Advance Confirmation: Because this service requires clinical assessment by senior staff, requests are subject to confirmation of appropriate clinical resources.
