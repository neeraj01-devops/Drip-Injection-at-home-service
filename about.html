Who We Are

Professional Clinical Care Where You Are Most Comfortable

Drip & Injection at Home Service operates as a specialized homecare coordination desk in Gurugram, Haryana. We bridge the gap between hospital discharge and full home recovery by coordinating licensed, qualified healthcare personnel directly to patient doorsteps.

Whether managing prescribed daily antibiotic injections, physician-ordered intravenous rehydration, sterile post-surgical wound care, Foley catheter management, or structured mobility physiotherapy, our team treats patient safety as the non-negotiable cornerstone of every home visit.

100% Prescription Verification: No injection or infusion is initiated without written medical authorization from a licensed clinician.
Aseptic Medical Protocol: Single-use sterile needles, sealed IV infusion sets, hospital-grade antiseptics, and biomedical waste disposal.
Attending Clinical Monitoring: Baseline vitals check (BP, pulse, SpO2) recorded before, during, and after therapy delivery.

Our Ethical Standards

Core Principles of Home Clinical Care

We operate strictly within clinician parameters. Dosages, administration routes (IM, SC, IV), and treatment timelines are cross-checked against your physician's prescription prior to dispatch.

Qualified Healthcare Staff

Care sessions are delivered by verified nurses and physiotherapists with clinical qualifications and experience handling aseptic techniques, cannulation, and patient mobilization.
