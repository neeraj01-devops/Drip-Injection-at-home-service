Who We Are

Bridging the Gap Between Hospital Discharge & Full Home Recovery

Drip & Injection at Home Service operates as a specialized homecare clinical coordination desk in Gurugram, Haryana. We support families during periods of medical convalescence by dispatching certified, experienced nursing personnel and physiotherapists directly to patient residences.

Whether a patient requires prescribed daily intramuscular antibiotic doses, physician-ordered intravenous saline or multivitamin infusions, sterile post-surgical wound dressing changes, catheter maintenance, or structured mobility rehabilitation, our clinical protocols ensure hospital-grade safety in comfortable domestic surroundings.

100% Prescription Validation: No injection, IV drip, or specialized infusion is administered without a verified written medical prescription from a licensed physician.
Hospital-Grade Aseptic Protocols: Strict hand hygiene, single-use disposable needles, sterile cannulas, hospital-standard antiseptics, and biomedical waste disposal.
Pre- & Post-Care Vitals Charting: Every session includes pre-assessment vitals (BP, heart rate, oxygen saturation) and ongoing monitoring until completion.
Emergency Triage Boundaries: Clear escalation protocols in place for sudden respiratory, cardiac, or allergic emergencies (immediate 112 / 102 coordination).

Patient Care Journey

How Our Home Clinical Service Works

A seamless, transparent 4-step protocol ensuring rapid response, medical verification, and clinical safety.

01

Prescription Intake

Share your doctor's written prescription via WhatsApp or Call. Our coordination desk reviews drug details, dosage, and scheduled administration route.

02

Nurse Dispatch

A verified, licensed nurse or physiotherapist is assigned and dispatched with sealed single-use clinical consumables tailored to your prescription.

03

Bedside Administration

The nurse records baseline vitals (BP, SpO2, Pulse), explains the procedure, and administers the therapy using aseptic hospital-grade protocols.

04

Observation & Charting

Patient is observed post-procedure for any adverse reactions, vital parameters re-checked, clinical documentation completed, and waste safely disposed.
